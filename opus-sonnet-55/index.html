Fifteen gap-filling units

Design study, not approved product requirements. Each unit fills a rule combination that no stock unit has. Click a column heading to sort, a unit name to open its dossier.

Overview

Range 2-3 means the unit fires at 2 or 3 hexes, never at 1. Reach is the farthest hex it can hit in one turn on open plains, with the road figure after it when different. Icons: Union, then Xenon, on the Legacy plains colour.
