Opening system

This applies to every selected board. Opening settings are saved with each tournament.

Each bot uses its own playing algorithm to try both opening roles before accepting a bonus. These bounded searches estimate preferences, not equal winning chances. Answers are private. Four games cover both faction assignments and both equal-offer tie breaks. Unequal bids decide the starting order; neither bot is forced to change its preference.
