museSpark1.3 — 15 Tactical Gap-Filling Units

Proposal and review pack: experimental tactical roster for Nectaris / Military Madness. Config schema in custom-units.json, full architectural dossiers in README.md. Run verification: node validate.js.

Engine Combat & Range Conventions: Range 1 is adjacent direct fire (allows defensive counterfire). Range 2 means strictly distance 2 (ring 2 indirect). Ranges 2–3, 2–4, etc. are indirect firing bands (blind spot at distance 1, no defensive counterfire). Turn policies: THEN = Move-then-fire; OR = Move-or-fire (setup required); RET = Attack-then-retreat (buggy hit-and-run); ST = Stationary deployable.
